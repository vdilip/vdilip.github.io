About

A bit more about me

Background

Where I started

I completed my Integrated Master's (BS-MS) in Earth and Climate Science at IISER Pune, graduating in 2019. My initial interest in understanding the causes of frequent droughts across the peninsular region of the Indian subcontinent drew me into this fascinating field of meteorology, and I have stayed here ever since.

Now

What I'm doing today

I have been pursuing my doctoral studies at IISER Pune since 2021, and my work has focused on understanding the causes of extreme heat events over the Indian subcontinent and developing adaptive strategies to help people cope with them.

Outside of work

Other interests

I go to the gym regularly when I'm not playing table tennis, cricket, or carrom. I also spend a lot of time feeding and caring for the dogs on campus, which brings me peace of mind after a hectic day. I enjoy exploring new restaurants and nearby places on my motorcycle (guilty as charged!).
