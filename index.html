PhD Student in Earth and Climate Science at the Indian Institute of Science Education and Research, Pune. I study the causes of extreme heat events and explore adaptation strategies for human health.

Overview

What I work on

By using statistics and time-series analysis applied to atmospheric reanalysis datasets such as ERA5, I aim to understand the causes of extreme heat events.

By developing simple Python models, I explore land-atmosphere interactions.

I am also interested in understanding dew/fog formation and its interactions with waves and instabilities in the atmospheric boundary layer.
